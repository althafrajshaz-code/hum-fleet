Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Effective Date: August 15, 2026

Hum Fleet is strictly committed to protecting children and ensuring our platforms remain free from Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). We have a zero-tolerance policy against any content or activity that exploits or endangers children.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

We do not permit any user, driver, or passenger to use the Hum Driver or Hum Passenger applications to distribute, create, or promote CSAE materials, or to engage in grooming, trafficking, or any behavior that threatens the safety of children.

2. Reporting and Action

  • Any user found to be violating this policy will have their account terminated immediately and permanently.
  • We actively monitor reports and will report any instances or suspicions of CSAE to the appropriate law enforcement agencies and organizations (such as the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children - NCMEC).

3. Commitment to Safety

Hum Fleet remains dedicated to fostering a safe environment for all our users. We continuously update our security practices and community guidelines to prevent the misuse of our platform for illicit activities regarding minors.

4. Contact Us

If you encounter or suspect any violations of this standard on our platform, please report it immediately to our support team.
