Privacy Policy for Hum Fleet

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Hum Fleet ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Hum Driver and Hum Passenger mobile applications. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Hum Fleet apps, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile picture (if provided).
  • Location Data: Real-time background and foreground location data to connect drivers with passengers, calculate ETAs, and track rides.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, and push notification tokens.
  • Financial Information: Payment details and wallet balances for ride processing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our ride-hailing services.
  • Match passengers with the nearest available drivers.
  • Process payments and maintain wallet balances securely.
  • Send ride updates, receipts, and important security alerts.

3. Location Data (Important for Drivers)

For the Hum Driver app, we collect background location data so you can receive ride requests even when the app is closed or not actively in use. This data is critical for assigning nearby rides.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share necessary information (like your first name, vehicle details, and real-time location) between drivers and passengers to facilitate a safe and successful ride.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your information is retained only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services and comply with legal obligations.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact our support team.
