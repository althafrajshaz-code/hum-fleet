Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Hum Fleet. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our mobile applications (Passenger and Driver apps) and website.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile picture when you register for an account.
  • Location Data: Precise or approximate location data from your mobile device to enable ride booking, navigation, and tracking features.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our apps, including ride history and preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our ride-booking services.
  • Connect passengers with drivers and facilitate navigation.
  • Process transactions and send related information.
  • Send support and administrative messages.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share your information:

  • Between Users: Passengers and drivers see each other's names, locations, and profile details to facilitate the ride.
  • With Service Providers: Third-party vendors that help us operate our services (e.g., hosting, maps, analytics).
  • For Legal Reasons: If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. Data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support section of the app.
